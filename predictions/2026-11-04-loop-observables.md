# Four more dated bets: what the loop implies

**Written on 4 November 2026.** Anthony Charretier and Claude, computed by `grm_loop.py` (part 4).

The loop without a seed (the full and the void in balance, |void| = |full|, equivalent to Koide's Q = 2/3) is one rule
with no free number. From the electron and the muon it fixes the tau; from the measured neutrino mass-squared
differences it fixes the three neutrino masses (normal order): **0.365, 8.685, 50.30 meV**
([2026-11-04-loop-neutrinos.md](2026-11-04-loop-neutrinos.md)). Everything below follows from these masses and the
measured mixing angles (PDG 2024: sin²θ₁₂ = 0.307 ± 0.013, sin²θ₁₃ = 0.0219 ± 0.0007). Each bet can refute the rule on
its own.

| # | quantity | the loop says | refuted if |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | tau mass | **1776.969 MeV** (from e and μ) | a measurement settles more than 3 sigma away (today: 1776.93 ± 0.09) |
| 2 | mass seen in beta decay, m_β = √(Σ\|U_ei\|² m_i²) | **8.84 meV** (8.69 to 8.99 with the angles' uncertainties) | a beta-decay experiment measures m_β above ~15 meV (today: < 450 meV, KATRIN 2025) |
| 3 | effective mass of neutrinoless double beta decay, m_ββ | **between 1.1 and 4.1 meV** (over the two unknown Majorana phases and the angles' uncertainties) | a signal is seen corresponding to m_ββ clearly above 4.1 meV |
| 4 | sum of the masses, seen by cosmology | **59.35 ± 0.30 meV** | a cosmological measurement settles clearly away from 59 meV (for example above 80 meV, or below 55 meV) |

Notes, so that nothing is read into them later:
- Bet 3 can only refute the loop: if no signal is ever seen, it may also mean that the neutrino is not its own
  antiparticle (then neutrinoless double beta decay does not happen at all).
- Bet 1 is Koide's old prediction, kept because it remains a test. If instead the phase were exactly 2/9, the tau would
  be 1776.967 MeV: the two cannot be told apart before a precision of ~0.002 MeV.
- The masses and the observables were computed before looking at any measurement newer than the PDG 2024 tables,
  except the first JUNO result checked for the Cantor bet ([2026-11-04-cantor-phase.md](2026-11-04-cantor-phase.md)).
