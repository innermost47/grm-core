# A dated bet: the neutrino masses from a loop without a seed

**Written on 4 November 2026**, before the measurements that will decide it. Anthony Charretier, with the GRM network
(`grm_loop.py`).

## The idea

Three waves are born from the void in opposition to the full, for ever, with no seed and no scale. Their amplitudes
r_n = √m_n split into the full (the part shared by all three) and the void (deviations that sum to zero). The loop:
neither wins, |void| = |full|. This is Koide's relation (Q = 2/3; the amplitudes at 45° from (1, 1, 1), Foot 1994,
arXiv:hep-ph/9402242). It holds for the charged leptons to 3 × 10⁻⁶: given the electron and the muon, it gives the tau at
1776.97 MeV (measured 1776.93 ± 0.09, PDG 2024).

## The bet

Applied to the neutrinos with the measured differences of their squared masses (PDG 2024: Δm²₂₁ = 7.53 × 10⁻⁵ eV²,
Δm²₃₂ = 2.455 × 10⁻³ eV² normal order, −2.529 × 10⁻³ eV² inverted), the loop closes only if one wave is in opposition
to the other two (an amplitude of the opposite sign: the idea of applying Koide to the neutrinos this way was proposed by
C. Brannen in 2006). It then gives:

| | m₁ | m₂ | m₃ | sum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **normal order** | **0.365 meV** | **8.685 meV** | **50.30 meV** | **59.35 ± 0.30 meV** |
| inverted order | 49.62 meV | 50.38 meV | 2.94 meV | 102.9 ± 0.6 meV |

The inverted solution is already refused by cosmology (DESI 2024 + CMB: sum < 72 meV at 95 %, arXiv:2404.03002).

**So the loop bets:**
1. the neutrino masses come in the **normal order** (JUNO and other oscillation experiments will tell);
2. their **sum is 59.35 ± 0.30 meV**, barely above the smallest sum the oscillations allow (≈ 59.0 meV): the lightest
   neutrino is almost massless, **m₁ ≈ 0.37 meV**.

## What would refute it

- the inverted order established by oscillation experiments;
- a cosmological sum measured below 59 meV, or clearly above 60 meV;
- a lightest neutrino mass measured well above 0.4 meV.

## What it would and would not show

If it holds, a relation with no free number (the full and the void in balance) would have predicted the masses of
particles nobody has weighed. If it fails, the loop is a property of the charged leptons only, and that too will be
known. Either way, nothing here is adjusted after the fact: this file is the bet as it was written.
